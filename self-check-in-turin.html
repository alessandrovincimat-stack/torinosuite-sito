Arrive any time between 3 pm and 9 pm: before you arrive you receive the instructions and a personal code to open the door.

If you expect to arrive after 9 pm, write to us before you set off.

How to get in

Before your arrival we write to you with the address, directions to the apartment and your personal door code, valid for your stay. There are no keys to collect and no reception desk: you let yourself in.

For every booking we need the details of all guests, which we register as required by law. Guests must be at least 18 years old unless accompanied.

House rules

  • No smoking.
  • Pets are not allowed.
  • Parties and events are not allowed.
  • Quiet hours from 11 pm to 8 am.
