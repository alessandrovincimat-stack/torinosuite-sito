Casa Borgone, apartment in Turin

55 m² apartment at Via Borgone 63, in a quiet residential area of Turin

Getting here and parking

Casa Borgone is at Via Borgone 63, in a quiet residential area. Metro line 1 is about 550 metres away, a 7-minute walk (Monte Grappa station), and connects to the city centre, Porta Susa and Porta Nuova. Bus stops are about 120 metres away. The Politecnico is about 2 km away.

The building has no parking: you can park on the street nearby, checking the signs. We recommend arriving by metro or public transport.

Useful questions

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is self-service, from 3:00 pm to 9:00 pm, with a personal code you receive before arrival. Check-out is by 10:30 am. If you expect to arrive after 9:00 pm, please let us know in advance.

Is there parking?

No, the building has no parking. You can park on the street nearby, checking the signs.

What is the cancellation policy?

For Casa Borgone, cancellation is free up to 14 days before arrival. The final conditions are the ones shown when you book.

Do I need an ID?

Yes. By law every guest must show a valid ID, for registration with the police (Questura).

Where is it?

At Via Borgone 63, 10139 Turin, in a different area from the other apartments, which are all at Via Carlo Alberto 31 in the historic centre.
