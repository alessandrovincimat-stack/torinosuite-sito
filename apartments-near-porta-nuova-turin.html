Apartments 700 metres from Porta Nuova station

Arriving by train? Porta Nuova station (trains and metro) is 700 metres from Via Carlo Alberto 31, and check-in is self-service: no keys to collect and no reception queue.

Before you arrive you receive instructions and a personal code to open the door: check-in is from 3:00 pm to 9:00 pm and check-out is by 10:30 am. You can walk from the station, and the historic centre begins right after.

If you arrive by car, note that central Turin has a limited-traffic zone (ZTL) and many paid-parking streets. The closest private garage is at Via Piero Gobetti 9, 200 metres from the apartments, staffed 24 hours a day (€41 or €48 per 24 hours depending on car size; information as of October 2026, prices and hours may change). Write to us before you travel: we will help you choose where to leave it.

Useful questions

How does check-in work?

Check-in is self-service, from 3:00 pm to 9:00 pm: before you arrive you receive instructions and a personal code to open the door, with no keys to collect. Check-out is by 10:30 am.

What is the cancellation policy?

For the Via Carlo Alberto apartments, cancellation is free up to 5 days before arrival. The final conditions are the ones shown when you book.

Do I need an ID?

Yes. By law every guest must show a valid ID at check-in, for registration with the police (Questura).
